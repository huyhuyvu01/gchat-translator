Privacy policy

Effective September 18, 2026.

Local Chat Translator reads Google Chat message text to add translation buttons. When you click Translate, Chrome's built-in Language Detector and Translator APIs process that message on your device.

The extension does not send message text or translations to the developer, a server, or a third-party translation service. It has no analytics, telemetry, advertising, accounts, or remote code.

Chrome may contact Google to download language models. Model downloads require an internet connection. Translation runs locally after the needed models are available. Chrome and Google Chat have their own privacy policies; this extension does not change how Google Chat stores or delivers your conversations.

For embedded Chat, the extension passes a selected message between its scripts in the same browser tab. This stays inside Chrome.

Only your enabled preference, target language, and source-language preference are saved in chrome.storage.local. Settings do not contain message text, translations, conversation identifiers, or browsing history and are not synced by the extension.

Translations stay in page memory and the visible page until the message is removed, settings change, or the page closes or reloads. Like other page content, inline results can be read by scripts with access to that page. Turning off translation buttons removes the extension's results from open Chat pages.

The storage permission saves settings. Scoped content scripts run on chat.google.com and mail.google.com/mail/ to place controls in Chat and support Gmail's embedded Chat. Gmail email messages are not translation targets. No tabs, cookies, history, webRequest, or all-sites permission is requested.

Uninstalling the extension removes its settings. To report a privacy issue, contact the maintainer through the source repository's security reporting channel. Do not include private conversations in public reports.
