Help & privacy

In Google Chat, hover over a message and click Translate. Your original message stays in place.

If short messages are detected incorrectly, choose a message language above.

The first translation may download Chrome's language models and ask you to retry. Requires desktop Chrome with built-in translation APIs.

Messages are translated on your device. Only your preferences are saved locally.
