Translate Google Chat messages on your device.

Help & privacy

Hover over a Google Chat message and click Translate. The result appears below the original.

If automatic detection gets the language wrong, choose the message language above.

Chrome may download language models on the first translation and ask you to retry. You need desktop Chrome with its built-in translation APIs.

Chrome translates on your device. The extension saves only your settings.
